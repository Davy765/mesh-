# Code of conduct

This project is meant to be a welcoming place to learn about and build decentralized AI, whatever your
background or level of experience.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with questions that seem basic.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people. Assume good intent.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Be honest about results, including failed experiments.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or repeatedly derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people from the
repository for behaviour that breaks this code. To report a problem, use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature, or contact the maintainer, @Davy765, through GitHub.
